HOW TO PLAY

Classic Hangman with a Bollywood & Hollywood twist!

1. The Goal: Guess the movie name one character at a time across 20 levels to win the game!
2. The Strike Rule: For every incorrect guess, one letter gets struck from BOLLYWOOD (if the movie is from Bollywood) or HOLLYWOOD (if from Hollywood). You have 9 strikes per movie.
3. Level Resets: Available guesses reset fresh for every level! Difficulty ramps up with every level from household blockbusters to rare cinephile gems.
4. Scoring System:
  • +10 points for every guessed movie.
  • +1 point for every unused guess left in that level (up to +9 bonus!).
5. Daily Leaderboard: Play everyday to build your daily streak, accumulate career points, and rise to the rank of Cinema Maestro!
