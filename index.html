HOW TO PLAY

Bollywood & Hollywood Cinema Guessing Game!

1. The Goal: Guess the movie name one character at a time. Available safe guesses reset fresh for every level!
2. The Strike Rule: For every incorrect guess, one letter gets struck from BOLLYWOOD or HOLLYWOOD (9 strikes allowed per movie).
3. Scoring: +10 points for guessing the movie + +1 point for every unused safe guess left!
4. ⚔️ Challenge Friends (5 Movies): Both of you guess the exact same 5 movies. The higher score wins a Purple Movie Ticket 🎟️💜!
5. 🎟️✨ Golden Movie Ticket: Survive and conquer all 20 levels in a single day to earn the prestigious Golden Movie Ticket!
